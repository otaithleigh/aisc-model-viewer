Commentary
==========

The available shear transfer strength at the bolt holes is the sum of the effective strength of the individual bolts which is the least of the following (User Note in AISC *Specification* Section J3.7).

1. Available bolt shear
2. Available bearing or tearout strength of the plate at the bolt hole
3. Available bearing or tearout strength of the beam web at the bolt hole

Bolt Shear
----------

Bolt shear is the limit state corresponding to shear failure of the bolt along
the shear plane (or interface). The strength is proportional to the number of
shear planes (interfaces).

In the case of the shear tab, there are two plies of material, and the bolt is
in single shear. There is one shear plane.

Bearing and Tearout Strength
----------------------------

Bearing strength is a measure of the strength of the material upon which a bolt
bears. It may be limited either by bearing deformation of the hole or by tearout
(a bolt-by-bolt block shear rupture) of the material upon which the bolt bears.
